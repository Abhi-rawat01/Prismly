# 💬 Love Score Calculator

> Analyze your chat conversations and calculate romantic potential based on messaging patterns.

## 🧮 How It Works

The Love Score is a **0-100% rating** based on your chat behavior. Every conversation starts at **50 points**, then factors are added or subtracted.

### 📊 Scoring Factors

- **⚡ Response Time** (up to +25): Faster replies = higher score
- **😊 Emoji Usage** (up to +20): More emojis = more engagement
- **⚖️ Message Balance** (up to +15): Equal participation is ideal
- **📅 Consistency** (up to +15): Regular chatting shows interest

### 💡 Score Guide

- **90-100%** 💖 Exceptional connection
- **75-89%** 💕 Great chemistry
- **60-74%** 💗 Good potential
- **40-59%** 💛 Moderate engagement
- **0-39%** 💔 Low engagement
